# 🍺 Cypher GM Taskbar v3.34.0

> *"Because GMs are people too. People who drink coffee at 2 AM and forget the NPC has 47 HP."*

Welcome to the **Cypher GM Taskbar** — the tool that will save you from looking like a clown in front of your players. (Although if you already are a clown, at least you'll be an *organized* clown.)

---

## 🎯 What Does This Do?

Imagine having an assistant who doesn't ask for pay, doesn't eat your pizza, and doesn't claim they "forgot" their dice. **That's this taskbar.**

Everything you need — at your fingertips. No jumping through 17 windows. No panic-searching chat logs. No more moments when a player says *"actually I have 15 Might pool, not 5"* and you know they're lying but have no proof.

---

## ✨ Features (Or: Why Your Players Will Be Jealous)

### 👥 Party Overview
Watch live pool bars for all PCs. See who's on death's door. See who's lying about their Effort. See who forgot they have a Damage Track status. **Knowledge is power. Power is fun.**

### ⚔️ Combat Tracker
Difficulty dial with TN. Combatant list. Round counter. Forget the *"uh, what was the order again?"* — now you know. The players don't. That's great.

### ⚡ GM Intrusions
Log and track intrusions with timestamps. Because nothing says *"I am an organized villain"* like a record of every moment you crushed your players' hopes and dreams.

### 🔮 Cyphers & Artifacts
Browse all cyphers by character. Finally see who's holding 47 cyphers and claiming they're "totally fine, boss, I only have 3." **Spoiler: they don't have 3.**

### 🛡️ Recovery Rolls
Track 1st / 2nd / 3rd / 4th recovery. Forget those moments when a player says *"I already recovered, didn't I?"* — now you have **proof**. Documented. Time-stamped. Undeniable.

### 👤 NPC Canvas
Tokens. Hover tooltips. Search. All your NPCs in one place, instead of wandering through folders like lost children at a fair.

### 📝 GM Notes
Quick notes that survive reloads. Write down *"don't forget to stab this guy in session 3"*. Or *"this NPC is actually the villain"*. Or *"buy milk"*. All entries are valid.

### 📢 CALL ROLL *(v3.17.32+ — serious business)*
The GM can trigger Cypher Taskbar roll dialogs directly on players' screens! Click **CALL ROLL** on anyone's pool — and BAM! The player's dialog opens. Like magic. But better. Because this actually works.
